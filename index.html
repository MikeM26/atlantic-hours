How it works

  1. Tell us about your trip. Dates, group, vibe and budget. It takes two minutes.
  2. Get your plan. We confirm the planning scope and timing, then send your itinerary and clear quote.
  3. Adjust it. Swap a restaurant, add a driver, change the pace until it feels right.
  4. Confirm with a deposit. The deposit amount and balance date are stated in your quote. Booking is confirmed in writing after payment and supplier confirmation.
  5. Arrive and enjoy. Your confirmation lists the arrangements and named contact for your booked experience.

Booking terms

Effective 28 October 2026

Atlantic Hours is based at 32 Kloof St, Gardens, Cape Town, 8000, South Africa. Contact us on WhatsApp +27 65 662 5625.

Your quote and confirmation

An enquiry is not a booking. Before payment, we provide a written quote identifying the experience, date, group size, inclusions, exclusions, supplier arrangements, total price, deposit, balance deadline and any specific cancellation conditions. We tell you whether Atlantic Hours supplies a service or arranges a booking with another provider. Availability is confirmed in writing after you accept the quote, pay the required reasonable deposit and the necessary supplier bookings are secured. If we cannot secure the agreed arrangements, you may choose an alternative or receive a refund of the amount paid for those unavailable arrangements.

Deposits and planning fees

The deposit amount depends on the arrangements and appears in your quote; it is credited against your total. We do not apply an automatic non-refundable deposit. A separate tailor-made design service begins only after you accept its scope, fee and delivery date. Any charge for planning already performed must have been disclosed, agreed and reasonable. Additional spend or itinerary changes require your approval before they are charged.

Cancellation by you

You may cancel an advance booking by contacting us in writing on WhatsApp. A reasonable cancellation charge may apply, taking account of the service, notice given, reasonable opportunity to resell the booking and relevant industry practice. We will explain the calculation and take reasonable steps to reduce the loss; supplier terms do not override your statutory rights. The refundable balance is returned. Where the Consumer Protection Act applies, no cancellation fee is charged when the booking cannot be honoured because of the death or hospitalisation of the person for whom it was made; reasonable supporting evidence may be requested.

Weather, changes and supplier cancellations

Outdoor activities depend on conditions and the operator’s safety decision. We cannot promise sunshine, a visible sunset or wildlife sightings. If an agreed activity cannot safely or lawfully proceed, we offer an available reschedule or suitable alternative for you to accept. A substantial change or extra charge requires your agreement. If you decline the alternative, or none is suitable, we refund the amount paid for the unavailable service. Services already delivered remain payable. We will not force a credit or voucher instead of a refund where you are entitled to money back.

Refunds and disputes

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where practicable. We aim to initiate the refund within 10 business days once the refundable amount and payment details are verified; your bank or payment provider may take additional time. This target does not reduce any shorter legal deadline. Contact Atlantic Hours on WhatsApp with your booking reference if an issue arises. Nothing in these terms excludes liability or limits rights that South African law does not allow us to exclude, including rights under the Consumer Protection Act.

Consumer guidance: Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008.

Privacy notice

Effective 28 October 2026

Responsible party and privacy contact: Atlantic Hours, 32 Kloof St, Gardens, Cape Town, 8000, South Africa. Contact Atlantic Hours on WhatsApp +27 65 662 5625 or call the same number for a privacy request.

What we collect and why

We use the name, WhatsApp contact details, travel dates, group size, interests, budget and preferences you choose to share to answer your enquiry, prepare a quote and arrange the services you request. Providing information is voluntary. Without sufficient dates, group details or a way to contact you, we may be unable to give a useful quote or fulfil a booking. We process information as needed to take steps at your request before a contract, perform a booking, meet legal obligations, or on another lawful basis under POPIA. We seek specific consent where required, including before using guest photographs or sending marketing. An enquiry is not marketing consent.

Your form and WhatsApp

The enquiry is composed in your browser. Atlantic Hours does not receive the form fields until you choose to send the prepared message through WhatsApp. Opening WhatsApp passes the prepared text to WhatsApp/Meta, which processes it under its own terms and privacy policy; messages and associated data may be processed outside South Africa. You may call instead if you prefer not to use WhatsApp. Do not enter identity numbers, card details, medical information or children’s details in the initial form. If special information is necessary for a requested service, we will explain the need, seek an appropriate lawful basis and collect only what is necessary.

Sharing and security

We share only relevant booking details with the transport, venue or activity providers needed for your agreed plan, and with professional advisers, payment providers or authorities where lawfully necessary. We do not sell personal information. We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards, limit access to people who need the information and require appropriate protection from service providers. Where we transfer information abroad, we apply a lawful POPIA section 72 basis, such as adequate protections, your informed consent or necessity for your requested booking. If a security compromise occurs, we take the notification and other steps required by POPIA.

Retention, cookies and the gallery

Enquiries and booking records are kept only as long as necessary for their purpose, legal record-keeping or a dispute, then securely deleted or de-identified. This site adds no advertising trackers or analytics cookies. The hosting provider may process technical logs, IP addresses and security-related information under its own privacy practices. The gallery editor previews selected photos in the editor’s browser; it does not upload them or publish them automatically. Do not include identifiable guests in a published gallery without appropriate permission.

Your rights

Subject to POPIA, you may ask whether we hold your information, request access or correction, request deletion where appropriate, object to processing or withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing or obligations that still apply. Contact Atlantic Hours at the number above; proportionate identity verification may be needed. You may complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator using its eServices portal or POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.

Further information: Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013.
