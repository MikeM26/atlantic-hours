Privacy notice

Effective 28 October 2026

Responsible party and privacy contact: Atlantic Hours, 32 Kloof St, Gardens, Cape Town, 8000, South Africa. Contact Atlantic Hours on WhatsApp +27 65 662 5625 or call the same number for a privacy request.

What we collect and why

We use the name, WhatsApp contact details, travel dates, group size, interests, budget and preferences you choose to share to answer your enquiry, prepare a quote and arrange the services you request. Providing information is voluntary. Without sufficient dates, group details or a way to contact you, we may be unable to give a useful quote or fulfil a booking. We process information as needed to take steps at your request before a contract, perform a booking, meet legal obligations, or on another lawful basis under POPIA. We seek specific consent where required, including before using guest photographs or sending marketing. An enquiry is not marketing consent.

Your form and WhatsApp

The enquiry is composed in your browser. Atlantic Hours does not receive the form fields until you choose to send the prepared message through WhatsApp. Opening WhatsApp passes the prepared text to WhatsApp/Meta, which processes it under its own terms and privacy policy; messages and associated data may be processed outside South Africa. You may call instead if you prefer not to use WhatsApp. Do not enter identity numbers, card details, medical information or children’s details in the initial form. If special information is necessary for a requested service, we will explain the need, seek an appropriate lawful basis and collect only what is necessary.

Sharing and security

We share only relevant booking details with the transport, venue or activity providers needed for your agreed plan, and with professional advisers, payment providers or authorities where lawfully necessary. We do not sell personal information. We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards, limit access to people who need the information and require appropriate protection from service providers. Where we transfer information abroad, we apply a lawful POPIA section 72 basis, such as adequate protections, your informed consent or necessity for your requested booking. If a security compromise occurs, we take the notification and other steps required by POPIA.

Retention, cookies and the gallery

Enquiries and booking records are kept only as long as necessary for their purpose, legal record-keeping or a dispute, then securely deleted or de-identified. This site adds no advertising trackers or analytics cookies. The hosting provider may process technical logs, IP addresses and security-related information under its own privacy practices. The gallery editor previews selected photos in the editor’s browser; it does not upload them or publish them automatically. Do not include identifiable guests in a published gallery without appropriate permission.

Your rights

Subject to POPIA, you may ask whether we hold your information, request access or correction, request deletion where appropriate, object to processing or withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing or obligations that still apply. Contact Atlantic Hours at the number above; proportionate identity verification may be needed. You may complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator using its eServices portal or POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.

Further information: Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013.
